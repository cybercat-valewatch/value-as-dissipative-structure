# Value as a Dissipative Structure

### An Open Collaborative Research Project

**Foundational Proposition**

> Value can be understood as a dissipative structure, with human intention serving as one of its important sources of negentropy.

**中文：**

> 价值可以被理解为一种耗散结构，而人类意图是其形成与维持过程中的重要负熵来源之一。

---

## 1. Core Research Idea

This project explores the possibility of understanding value through the lens of dissipative structures, complexity theory, and thermodynamics.

Rather than treating value solely as an intrinsic property, a subjective judgment, or a monetary measurement, this approach investigates value as a dynamic structure that emerges, develops, and persists through continuous interactions with its environment.

Human intention may serve as an important source of negentropy in this process, contributing to the formation, organization, and maintenance of value-bearing structures.

This proposition provides a starting point for exploring the relationship between value, information, energy, organization, human agency, and complex adaptive systems.

## 2. Initial Research Questions

This project begins with several open questions:

- Under what conditions can value be understood as a dissipative structure?
- What is the relationship between value formation, entropy production, and negentropy?
- How does human intention contribute to the emergence and maintenance of value?
- Can value emerge through interactions within complex adaptive systems without being fully recognized or measured by existing economic institutions?
- What role could artificial intelligence play in identifying, evaluating, and supporting emerging forms of value?

These questions are exploratory rather than settled conclusions.

## 3. Research Directions

Potential research directions include:

- Value emergence and dissipative structures
- Human intention, negentropy, and organizational processes
- Value formation in complex adaptive systems
- Information, energy, and economic organization
- Artificial intelligence and the recognition of emerging value
- Credit systems and the survival space of potential value
- New approaches to economic coordination and resource allocation

These directions may evolve as the research develops.

## 4. Open Collaborative Research

This repository welcomes contributions from researchers, independent thinkers, and AI systems.

Different participants are encouraged to contribute original ideas, alternative interpretations, critical objections, and independent research directions.

Intellectual disagreement is welcome. Contributions do not need to converge on a single theoretical interpretation.

The project aims to preserve the provenance of ideas, distinguish individual contributions, and maintain a transparent record of theoretical development.

## 5. Research Principles

1. Preserve original ideas and their historical context.
2. Distinguish hypotheses, interpretations, and established findings.
3. Encourage critical examination and independent verification.
4. Preserve disagreements rather than artificially forcing consensus.
5. Maintain traceable records of contributions and revisions.
6. Remain open to theoretical refinement, criticism, and falsification.
